# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying.

This project exists so people can learn how a database works. Someone asking a basic question is the
audience, not a nuisance.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments. Dismissing someone for not knowing something.
Sexualised language or imagery. Publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Report anything that needs attention by opening an issue or contacting the maintainer directly.
Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, code, and
issues that do not follow this, and may ban repeat offenders.
